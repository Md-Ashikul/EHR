# Source Verification Report: Lee & Choi (2021) Paper Analysis

## Question
Does the Lee & Choi (2021) paper "Hospital Productivity After Data Breaches" contain the statistic:
> "Over 276 million individuals had their protected health records exposed or stolen in a single year, with healthcare breaches averaging $7.42 million per incident"

---

## Answer: ❌ NO - This Information is NOT in the Lee & Choi (2021) Paper

### What the Lee & Choi (2021) Paper Actually Contains

The paper focuses on **hospital productivity metrics** (value added) after data breaches in California hospitals (2012-2016). It does NOT provide those specific statistics.

What the paper DOES cite regarding breach costs:

**From Introduction Section (p. 2):**
- "In 2019, the average total cost of a data breach for all industries globally was US $3.92 million"
- "The average total cost for all industries in the United States was US $8.19 million"
- "The cost per breached record averages US $242 in the US and US $150 globally"
- "In US health care industry, per-record breaches cost an average of US $429"

**Source cited**: Reference [8] - Ponemon L. "What's New in the 2019 Cost of a Data Breach Report." SecurityIntelligence. 2019.

---

## Where Do Those Statistics ("276 million" + "$7.42 million") Come From?

The statistics you mentioned appear to come from **different sources**, likely:

### Possible Source 1: HHS/OCR Data
- The Lee & Choi paper references HHS Breach Portal data
- Recent OCR reports (2024) show **242 million records** exposed in large breaches
- This is CLOSE to 276 million but NOT exactly the same

### Possible Source 2: Ponemon Report (IBM Security)
- The paper cites Ponemon's 2019 report
- However, Ponemon tracks **average cost per incident**, not total individuals affected
- Latest Ponemon 2024 report shows varying healthcare costs per record

### Possible Source 3: Different Year/Publication
- The "276 million" + "$7.42 million" figures may be from:
  - A different Ponemon year (2020, 2021, etc.)
  - HIPAA Journal statistics
  - Healthcare IT News reports
  - Different research paper altogether
