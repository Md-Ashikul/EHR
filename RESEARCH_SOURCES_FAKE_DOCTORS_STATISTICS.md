## 1. BANGLADESH - Critical Statistics

### Key Finding: 54% of Population Seeks Care from Unqualified Providers

**Source**: Bangladesh Bureau of Statistics (BBS) - Health and Morbidity Status Survey (HMSS) 2025
- **Survey Details**: 189,986 individuals across 47,040 households (November-December 2025)
- **Finding**: **54% of patients sought treatment from drugstore salespersons or practiced self-medication**
- **Comparison**: Only 11.5% visited government health facilities, 20% turned to private providers
- **Publication**: The Business Standard (December 2025)
- **DOI**: Direct government survey data
- **Link**: https://publisher.tbsnews.net/bangladesh/health/half-population-bangladesh-seeks-care-unqualified-providers-survey-1298926

---

### Village Doctor Problem: Nearly 95% of Healthcare Workforce

**Source**: ICDDR,B (International Centre for Diarrhoeal Disease Research, Bangladesh)
**Publication**: "Doctoring the Village Doctors: Giving Attention Where it is Due" (2011)
**Official Publisher**: ICDDR,B & UK Department for International Development (DFID)

**Key Statistics**:
- **Nearly 95% of the entire national health workforce in Bangladesh comprises informal sector providers**
- **Village doctors (VDs) lack proper accreditation or certified medical knowledge**
- **Inappropriate drug prescribing**: Only 18.4% appropriate, 7.1% harmful, 74.5% unnecessary
- **Case study**: Review of 89 treatment cases showed significant deficiencies in care quality

**Citation Format**:
> Bhuiya, A., Rasheed, S., Wahed, T. (2011). Doctoring the Village Doctors: Giving Attention Where it is Due. ICDDR,B: Centre for Health and Population Research, Bangladesh.

**ISBN**: 978-984-551-332-6

---

### Shortage of Qualified Medical Professionals

**Source**: Bangladesh Health Watch & Government of Bangladesh Health Ministry

**Statistics**:
- **Shortage of 60,000 physicians** (against current 38,537)
- **Shortage of 280,000 nurses** (against current 15,023)
- **18,000 vacant positions** in public healthcare (including ~5,500 doctor posts)
- **Doctor absenteeism**: 40% regularly absent at Upazila Health Centres, 74% absent at Union Health Centres

**Impact**: This supply-demand gap forces patients toward unqualified providers

## 6. WHO Global Context

**Source**: World Health Organization - Global Strategy on Human Resources for Health

**Key Points**:

- Shortage of qualified healthcare workers endemic in South-East Asia Region
