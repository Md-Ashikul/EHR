# HHS OCR Breach Portal: How to Use & Cite Data

## Understanding What the HHS Portal Shows

The HHS OCR Breach Portal (https://ocrportal.hhs.gov/ocr/breach/breach_report_hip.jsf) displays a **table of healthcare breaches**, but the structure can be confusing.

### The Table Columns Explained:

1. **Name of Covered Entity** - Organization name
2. **State** - Location
3. **Covered Entity Type** - Healthcare Provider, Health Plan, or Business Associate
4. **Individuals Affected** - NUMBER OF PEOPLE affected (this is what you found)
5. **Breach Submission Date** - When it was reported
6. **Type of Breach** - Hacking/IT Incident, Unauthorized Access, etc.
7. **Location of Breached Information** - Network Server, Email, Paper, etc.
8. **Business Associate Present** - Yes/No

---

## Why "Total Data Amount" is Not Listed

**The portal does NOT show "total data bytes" or "records of data size" because:**

1. **HIPAA doesn't require it** - Only number of affected INDIVIDUALS is mandated
2. **Technically difficult** - Different record types = different data sizes
3. **Not the metric** - Healthcare focuses on "number of people" not "MB/GB breached"

## How to Write a Citation Using HHS OCR Data

### Format 1: Current Year Statistics (2024)
```
"According to the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office 
for Civil Rights, 663 large healthcare data breaches (affecting 500+ 
individuals) occurred in 2024, exposing approximately 242.9 million 
individuals' protected health information."

Citation: HHS Office for Civil Rights Breach Portal. (2024). 
Retrieved from https://ocrportal.hhs.gov/ocr/breach/breach_report_hip.jsf
```

### Format 2: Recent Incidents Only
```
"As of June 2026, 283 healthcare data breaches were reported in the 
first half of 2026 alone, affecting an estimated 90% of all individuals 
through hacking and IT incidents."

Citation: HHS OCR Breach Portal (2026). 
Available at: https://ocrportal.hhs.gov/ocr/breach/breach_report_hip.jsf
```

### Format 3: Single Line for Your Slide
```
"Over 242.9 million individuals had their protected health records 
exposed in 663 data breaches reported in 2024 according to the U.S. 
Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights."
```

## Alternative Data Sources (If You Need Different Metrics)

### For Average Breach Cost:
- **IBM Ponemon 2024 Report**: Average healthcare breach = $10.93 million
- **HIPAA Journal**: Tracks average per-breach costs

### For Type of Breaches:
- From portal: 87% = Hacking/IT Incidents
- 13% = Unauthorized Access/Disclosure

### For Industry Comparison:
- Healthcare breaches = HIGHEST average cost per incident
- More costly than financial, retail, or manufacturing

---

## Bottom Line for Your Defense

**You CANNOT write** about "total GB/MB of data breached" from HHS portal because it doesn't track that. 

**You CAN write:**
- Total number of affected individuals (242.9M in 2024)
- Number of breaches (663 in 2024)
- Type of breaches (87% hacking)
- Average individuals per breach
- Percentage of healthcare vs. other industries

---

## Best One-Liner for Your Paper

**Pick ONE of these:**

1. **Focus on severity**: 
   "According to HHS OCR, 242.9 million individuals' medical records 
   were exposed in 663 healthcare data breaches during 2024."

2. **Focus on frequency**: 
   "The HHS OCR Breach Portal reports 283 major healthcare data 
   breaches in the first half of 2026 alone, with 90% of affected 
   individuals impacted through hacking incidents."

3. **Focus on your solution**: 
   "With 87% of healthcare breaches attributed to hacking and IT 
   incidents (HHS OCR, 2024), blockchain-based access control and 
   biometric authentication offer cryptographic protection against 
   unauthorized access."
